WHAT TO VERIFY FIRST
For your particular brief, the most useful next step is not buying the largest curriculum. It is testing whether a provider can show the full chain: Search demand → discoverability → activated users → paid conversion → retained subscriptions → profit.
Before paying, request:
1. Three named student products with live store/website links.
2. Student confirmation that they took the specific program being sold now.
3. Revenue definitions: gross receipts, MRR, net revenue, or profit.
4. Acquisition breakdown: Google SEO, store search, social, ads, partnerships, or outreach.
5. Costs: AI usage, hosting, store accounts, refunds, payment fees, and revenue share.
6. A cohort denominator: enrolled, launched, first payment, still operating.
7. Ownership and portability: source code, accounts, customer data, and billing.
8. Current technical coverage: not merely an old successful instructor project.
9. Written refund and cancellation terms for the exact package.
10. One sample lesson on distribution, not only an impressive AI-building demo.
