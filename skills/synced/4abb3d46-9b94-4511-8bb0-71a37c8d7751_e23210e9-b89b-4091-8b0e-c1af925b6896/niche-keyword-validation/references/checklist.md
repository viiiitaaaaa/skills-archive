# Rapid screening checklist

## Step 0 — fix the phrasing

Look the keyword up in a planner and scan related phrasings for volume before checking anything else. Trend tools rank by growth, not volume, so the phrase they display is often a minor variant of a far larger keyword they never show. `lamp warmer` = 1,300/month; `candle lamp warmer` = 110,000/month. Validate the large one.

## Pass / fail

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Competition (planner) | Low / Medium | 100, or "High" |
| Query intent | best, what type, do I need | DIY, cheap, ideas, how to make |
| Brands in top results | none, or 1–2 weak | manufacturer names at the top |
| Trajectory (smoothed, 5y) | rises across full period | flat for years, then recent spike |
| Interest level / volume | above ~1,000; interest ≥ 20 | "Undetermined"; interest 1–5 |
| Geography | several regions, sensible pattern | one region at 100, rest blank |
| Related trends panel | populated | empty |
| Phrasing | action, audience, or material | bare category term |

Competition, intent and brand occupancy are disqualifiers — any one fails and the rest doesn't matter. Trajectory and volume are the minimum bar. The remainder shape attractiveness.

## Reading the query list

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| material qualifiers: steel, hydrocolloid, silicone, wooden, merino | **Best positive signal.** Buyers understand properties, will pay for better |
| action or audience phrasing | Real, articulated demand |
| DIY, cardboard, homemade, cheap, ideas | Traffic without buyers |
| best, what level, do I need | Ready to buy, choosing |
| does it work, is it legit, side effects | Category distrusted — must sell proof first |
| store names, near me | Buying at known retailers, not independents |
| SHEIN, AliExpress dominant | Price race — nothing to compete on |
| brand names dominant | Occupied regardless of trend |
| FDA approved, insurance codes (hcpc, cpt) | Ad rejection and compliance ahead |

## Market-stage indicators

- **"best" rising** → open, buyers seek advice, interceptable with content
- **"best" falling + brands rising** → closed, choice made for them
- **"reddit" queries falling** → same signal, nobody asks advice anymore
- **former leader falling, challengers rising** → price competition begun
- **verdicts don't transfer between synonyms** — test each phrasing separately

## Baseline decline rate

The headline "past month" figure is meaningless alone. Establish the baseline with a stable staple (paper towels, toothpaste) in the same session, then compare.

If the staple shows roughly −25%:

| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| better than −15% | outperforming baseline — genuine strength |
| −20% to −30% | matching baseline — neutral |
| worse than −40% | real decline |

Recompute whenever the tool or period changes.

## Data caveats

- **Spring 2026 lift** appears across unrelated keywords in both Glimpse and Google Trends, visible even on stable staples. Compare August to last August, never to the May peak.
- **Placeholder exports** — all cells reading 88,888 / 8.88 mean no data access. The keyword list still works and often tells more than the numbers would.
- **Percentages between slices aren't comparable** — normalization differs by root query.
- **Ambiguous terms derail the slice.** "caddy" returns shower and diaper organizers; "stand" returns KitchenAid mixers; "mat" returns yoga mats; "tool" returns construction. Re-query with an unambiguous phrase.

## Advertising-spike signatures

- Brand-name queries: `is X legit`, `does X work`, `X reviews`
- Multiple unrelated keywords in one category spiking the same month
- Four-figure growth on a keyword with negligible interest
- Flat multi-year history ending in a vertical rise

## Before dropping a niche

Re-test the same product phrased as:

1. An action — "for flying", "for swelling"
2. An audience — "for nurses", "for runners"
3. A material — "stainless steel", "hydrocolloid", "silicone"
4. A symptom rather than a diagnosis — "swelling" not "lymphedema"
5. A specific product type — "rake brush" not "dog brush"

Only after all five fail is the niche genuinely dead.
